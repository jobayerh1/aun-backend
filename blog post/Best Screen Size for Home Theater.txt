<h2 id="distance">1) Viewing distance → choose your screen size</h2>
        <p>For a comfortable “cinema feel,” sit about <strong>1.5× screen width</strong> away.</p>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>80″</strong> diagonal ≈ 1.77 m wide → seat ≈ <strong>2.6 m</strong></li>
          <li><strong>100″</strong> diagonal ≈ 2.21 m wide → seat ≈ <strong>3.3 m</strong></li>
          <li><strong>120″</strong> diagonal ≈ 2.65 m wide → seat ≈ <strong>4.0 m</strong></li>
        </ul>
        <p><em>Tip:</em> If your room is small, pick 80–100″ and improve black-out/lighting; you’ll get better contrast and comfort.</p>

        <h2 id="throw">2) Throw distance → make sure it fits</h2>
        <p><strong>Quick math:</strong> Screen width × <em>throw ratio</em> ≈ projector distance.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Common throw ratios for home models: <strong>~1.2–1.5</strong></li>
          <li>Example (100″): screen width 2.21 m × 1.2–1.5 → projector at <strong>2.6–3.3 m</strong></li>
        </ul>
        <p>Check the spec sheet for the exact throw ratio of your model to avoid keystone and focus issues — every AUN product page also has a built-in <strong>throw-distance calculator</strong> that does this math for your room automatically.</p>
<p>You can review throw ratios and specifications from our updated <a href="/projector-price/">projector price list in Bangladesh</a> before deciding your final screen size.</p>
