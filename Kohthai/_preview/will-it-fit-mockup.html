“Will it fit” — how to draw the bag

Retro Artistic Style Embroidered Crossbody Bag, at the module’s real geometry.

Measured from the live page: body box 210 × 160 mm · photograph drawn 260 × 275 mm · overflow 110 mm above (handle), 46 mm right (scarf). Scene, scale and dashed box are identical in all three — only the photograph is treated differently.

1. Nowhard crop

The photograph is cut off exactly on the dashed box. The scarf is sliced by a straight edge and the handle is gone. This is what you are seeing now.

2. Faded strapsrecommended

The whole bag is there, so nothing looks broken — but only the part the numbers describe is solid. No hard cut anywhere, and the handle fades out instead of being chopped, so no product needs a bigger frame.

3. Full bag, solidnot advised

The silhouette now reads as the product — and it is 1.24× wider and 1.72× taller than the 21 × 16 cm you sell. The bag looks bigger than it is, and the phone against the scarf makes the verdict look arbitrary. Also note the handle is still cut, just by the panel edge instead.

Everything above uses the real photograph, the real body box and the real scene box taken from the live module — nothing is redrawn by hand, so what you see is what the change would produce.

The green rectangle is an iPhone 16 Pro Max at true scale, turned sideways, as the fit test would place it. The from the side depth panel is unchanged by any of this.
