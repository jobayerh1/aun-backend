## 7. Answers to your questions

**Public or unlisted on YouTube?**

Both are accepted for a Play store listing. **Choose public.**

Unlisted only makes sense if you do not want the video found. This is a launch
announcement — you want it searchable, shareable on WhatsApp and Facebook,
and sitting on your channel where someone researching AUN will find it. An
unlisted video does none of that, and it gains you nothing on the listing.

Before you paste the URL into Play Console:
- Visibility: **Public**
- Monetization: **off**
- Embedding: **allowed** (Play requires it)
- Not age-restricted, not a Short, not a live video
- Use the full `youtube.com/watch?v=...` URL, with no tracking parameters
- Do not use a playlist or channel URL

Upload the **16:9** file. The 9:16 version would be classified as a Short, and
Play does not accept Shorts for listings.

**One more thing worth doing:** set a custom thumbnail. The feature graphic
(1024x500) works, or a frame from the video. YouTube's auto-pick will often
land on a dark transition frame.
