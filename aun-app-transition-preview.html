Spatial spring — things that move

stiffness 380 · damping 0.82. Position and scale ride this. It overshoots about 1% and settles, which is what gives motion weight instead of a mechanical glide.

Effects spring — colour & opacity

stiffness 3800 · damping 1.0. Critically damped, so a fade can never wobble — an opacity bounce reads as a rendering bug, and opacity has no inertia to justify one. This split is the core of the M3 Expressive motion system.

Why it matches the system

The covered page scales to 92%, drifts 24 px, dims 25% and rounds to 28 px — Android’s predictive-back preview. Tap-navigation and back-gesture finally produce the same shape, so the app stops looking like it has two animation systems.
