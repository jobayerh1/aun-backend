# 🔴 Fix this line before anything else

> *"designed for the modern and **middle-aged** woman and who values both style and practicality"*

**Never tell a customer she is middle-aged.** No fashion brand on earth does it, and this is the
second sentence she reads. A 45-year-old woman does not want to be sold a bag *because* she is 45;
she wants the same thing a 25-year-old wants — for it to look good and hold her things.

The bag may genuinely be designed with an older customer in mind. **You still never say it.** You
say "organised", "practical", "everything has its place" — and the right customer recognises
herself without being labelled.

It also has a grammar error — *"woman and who values"* — which on its own undermines a ৳2,550
product.

**This is the single most costly sentence I have seen in your catalogue**, because unlike a wrong
weight it turns customers away silently and you never find out.

## One thing worth thinking about — not urgent, and not a warning

This bag has a **metal logo plaque on the front reading "KAIDIPEINIROO"**, with a kangaroo emblem.

**To be clear: this is not the Neverfull situation.** That is a real Chinese brand's own product,
legitimately bought and legitimately resold. Nothing about it is counterfeit and there is nothing
to fix urgently.

But it is worth noticing, for two reasons:

**1. Your customer receives a bag branded as somebody else.** She ordered from Kohthai. The
plaque is visible in your photos, so nobody is being misled — but your copy never mentions it, and
"100% Genuine" in your trust row invites the question *genuine what?*

**2. Every customer carrying this bag is advertising KAIDIPEINIROO, not Kohthai.** You told me the
goal is *"a trendy bag company for women."* A bag company's logo is on its bags.

⭐ **And there is a cheap fix available.** On the Rodeo bag's 1688 listing you sent me, the supplier
offered **"Labeling/box mark +¥0.5"** as a standard packaging option — about **৳7 per bag**. Most
of these factories will do OEM labelling, your own dust bag, or an unbranded version, at low
volume. It is worth one message to ask.

That is a decision for when you are ready, not today. But at some point "Kohthai" has to appear on
a Kohthai bag.
