=== Kohthai Campaign Notice Bar ===
Contributors: smartliving
Tags: campaign, notice, banner, shortcode, flatsome, woocommerce, countdown
Requires at least: 5.8
Tested up to: 6.6
Requires PHP: 7.4
Stable tag: 2.0.0

Scheduled campaign notices for Kohthai. Shortcodes:
  [kohthai_campaign_notice]          -- the top bar
  [kohthai_campaign_product_notice]  -- the product page notice

Settings: Settings -> Kohthai Campaign Bar

v2.0.0 rebuilds the plugin on the AUN Campaign Notice Bar v1.8.3 engine. The
shortcodes and the option key are unchanged, so existing pages and saved settings
carry straight over. See the header of kohthai-campaign-bar.php for the full
changelog, and DEPLOY-kohthai-campaign-bar.md for upgrade steps.
