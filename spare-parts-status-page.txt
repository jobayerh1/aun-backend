<details><summary><span>What do the statuses mean?</span><i class="fa-solid fa-chevron-down aun-chev"></i></summary><div class="aun-faq-a"><strong>Quote sent</strong> &mdash; a price is waiting for your approval. <strong>Approved</strong> &mdash; you&rsquo;ve approved and we&rsquo;re sourcing the parts. <strong>Pending</strong> &mdash; received and being reviewed. <strong>At factory</strong> &mdash; we&rsquo;ve ordered it. <strong>Shipped / Arrived</strong> &mdash; on the way to or received by us. <strong>Dispatched</strong> &mdash; on the way to you. <strong>Delivered</strong> &mdash; complete. Parts are tracked one by one, so some may move faster than others.</div></details>
<details><summary><span>You sent me a quote &mdash; how do I approve it?</span><i class="fa-solid fa-chevron-down aun-chev"></i></summary><div class="aun-faq-a">Enter your SP- number or phone above. When a quote is ready you&rsquo;ll see each part&rsquo;s price, the total and payment details, with <strong>Approve</strong> and <strong>Decline</strong> buttons. Tap <strong>Approve</strong> and we&rsquo;ll start sourcing your parts right away.</div></details>

<details><summary><span>How long will my parts take?</span><i class="fa-solid fa-chevron-down aun-chev"></i></summary><div class="aun-faq-a">In-stock parts dispatch quickly. Parts ordered from the factory usually take 3&ndash;4 weeks. The status here always shows the current stage.</div></details>
