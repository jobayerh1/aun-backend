<h2>2) Size & distance (quick math)</h2>
        <ul>
          <li>Screen width × 1.2–1.5 ≈ throw distance</li>
          <li>100″ screen ≈ 2.2 m wide → distance ≈ 2.4–3.3 m</li>
          <li>Seat ~1.5× screen width away for comfort</li>
        </ul>
[/ux_html]

<h2>3) Screen & light</h2>
        <ul>
          <li>Dark rooms: matte white fixed/pull-down screens work great</li>
          <li>Bright rooms: consider higher-gain or ALR; close blinds</li>
          <li>Avoid glossy walls; smooth light-grey works if no screen</li>
        </ul>
[/ux_html]

<h2>4) Sound & seating</h2>
        <ul>
          <li>Soundbar is the easiest upgrade; place below the screen</li>
          <li>For bigger rooms: 2.1/3.1 bookshelf or tower speakers</li>
          <li>Keep seating centered; avoid blocking the beam path</li>
        </ul>
