Your own description already says so: *"underarm bag"*, *"hobo design"*, *"85cm shoulder strap"*.

slider buckle. A tote has two handles and a structured rectangular body. This has neither.

**But the title says Tote, and it is filed in your Tote Bag category.**

That costs you in three directions at once:

1. A customer browsing **Tote Bag** finds something that is not a tote and leaves.
