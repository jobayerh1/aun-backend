## ⭐ Where these came from — worth knowing for next time

Both wrong descriptions are exact copies of **two specific products**:

```
Elegant Flap PU Leather Crossbody  ->  leaked onto  Versatile Soft PU Leather Shoulder Bag
Stylish PU Leather Crossbody       ->  leaked onto  Retro Artistic Embroidered Crossbody
                                                    (and onto the Rodeo bag, now fixed)
```

So **Stylish PU Leather Crossbody is the product you duplicate from most often** — its description
has escaped onto two others. When you next duplicate a product, that is the field to clear first.

⭐ **A quicker way to catch this in future:** search Google for
`site:kohthaibd.com "a distinctive phrase from the description"`. If two of your products come
back, one of them is wrong. "scrunchie handle" would have found this in ten seconds.
