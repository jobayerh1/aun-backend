[accordion-item title="Which projector screen is best for a bright room?"]
For rooms with daylight or the lights on, choose an ALR (Ambient Light Rejecting) screen. The White Grid ALR gives a bright image across a wide 160° angle, while the Gray ALR delivers deeper contrast in a focused 20° sweet spot. Plain white and portable screens look their best in a dim or dark room, or outdoors after dark.
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="What is an ALR (anti-light) projector screen?"]
An ALR — Ambient Light Rejecting — screen is built to reject stray room light instead of reflecting it, so your picture stays bright and high-contrast even when the room isn't fully dark. It's the best choice for living rooms and any space you can't black out completely.
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="ALR screen vs portable white screen — which should I buy?"]
Buy an ALR screen if your room has ambient light and you want the best contrast and brightness. Buy a portable white screen if you want an affordable, foldable screen for a dark room or outdoor movie nights. ALR screens cost a little more; portable screens are the budget-friendly, go-anywhere option.
[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="What projector screen sizes are available?"]
