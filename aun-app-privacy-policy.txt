<h2 style="font-size:22px;margin:34px 0 8px;color:#0f172a;"><i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved" style="color:#16a34a;margin-right:8px;"></i>2. What the app does <em>not</em> collect</h2>
<ul style="font-size:14.5px;color:#475569;line-height:1.9;margin:0;padding-left:20px;">
<li><strong>No location.</strong> The app never requests or reads your location. Weather shown on the home screen is for Dhaka generally, worked out on our server — your phone is never asked where it is.</li>
<li><strong>No contacts, no call logs, no SMS reading.</strong> When Android auto-fills your one-time code, it hands the app only that single message, using Google's SMS Retriever. We cannot read your other messages.</li>
<li><strong>No advertising, no advertising ID, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Camera stays on your phone.</strong> The camera is used only to scan the barcode on your projector box. The picture is processed on the device and is never uploaded or stored.</li>
<li><strong>No payments in the app.</strong> The current version does not take payments and does not handle card details.</li>
</ul>

<p style="font-size:13.5px;color:#64748b;line-height:1.7;margin:16px 0 0;">The "what to watch" film suggestions come from <strong>The Movie Database (TMDB)</strong>, and video guides play from <strong>YouTube</strong>. Film suggestions are fetched by our server, not your phone — TMDB receives nothing about you. Playing a video is a normal YouTube view and is subject to Google's privacy policy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of our customers.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:22px;margin:34px 0 8px;color:#0f172a;"><i class="fa-solid fa-clock-rotate-left" style="color:#0188fe;margin-right:8px;"></i>4. How long we keep it</h2>
<ul style="font-size:14.5px;color:#475569;line-height:1.9;margin:0;padding-left:20px;">
<li><strong>Your app profile and sign-in</strong> — until you delete your account. A sign-in session expires by itself after 180 days.</li>
<li><strong>One-time codes</strong> — a few minutes, and they are stored hashed, never as plain numbers.</li>
<li><strong>Warranty registrations and sales records</strong> — kept for as long as the warranty and our accounting obligations require, because they are your proof of purchase and our transaction record. These survive account deletion (see section 5).</li>
<li><strong>Repair and spare-parts records</strong> — kept as service history; after account deletion they are detached from your account.</li>
<li><strong>Support tickets</strong> — kept in our support system as a record of the conversation.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="font-size:22px;margin:34px 0 8px;color:#0f172a;"><i class="fa-solid fa-user-minus" style="color:#7c3aed;margin-right:8px;"></i>5. Your choices and your rights</h2>
<ul style="font-size:14.5px;color:#475569;line-height:1.9;margin:0;padding-left:20px;">
<li><strong>See and correct your information</strong> — your profile is editable in the app at any time (tap your photo on the home screen, or Settings).</li>
<li><strong>Delete your account and personal data</strong> — in the app: <strong>Settings → Delete my account</strong>. Full details, including exactly what is deleted and what we must keep, are on our <a href="/delete-account/" style="color:#0188fe;font-weight:600;">Delete your account</a> page. You can also ask us to do it for you.</li>
<li><strong>Turn notifications off</strong> — in the app, or in your phone's Android settings. Nothing else stops working.</li>
<li><strong>Remove a projector</strong> — remove it in My Devices; we remember the choice so it is not added back automatically.</li>
<li><strong>Ask us a question or complain</strong> — use any contact route below. We answer in Bangla or English.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="font-size:22px;margin:34px 0 8px;color:#0f172a;"><i class="fa-solid fa-lock" style="color:#0188fe;margin-right:8px;"></i>6. How we protect it</h2>
<p style="font-size:14.5px;color:#475569;line-height:1.7;margin:0;">All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. One-time codes are stored hashed, never in readable form, and are rate-limited against guessing. Sign-in tokens are stored hashed, are tied to the phone that signed in, expire after 180 days, and are destroyed the moment you log out or delete your account. Access to customer data inside our systems is limited to staff who need it for support, service and warranty work. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously and fix problems quickly when they are reported.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:22px;margin:34px 0 8px;color:#0f172a;"><i class="fa-solid fa-child-reaching" style="color:#0188fe;margin-right:8px;"></i>7. Children</h2>
<p style="font-size:14.5px;color:#475569;line-height:1.7;margin:0;">The app is intended for customers who own an AUN projector — that is, adults making a purchase. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:22px;margin:34px 0 8px;color:#0f172a;"><i class="fa-solid fa-globe" style="color:#0188fe;margin-right:8px;"></i>8. Where your data is stored</h2>
<p style="font-size:14.5px;color:#475569;line-height:1.7;margin:0;">Our servers and service providers may be located outside Bangladesh. Wherever your data is processed, this policy applies to it and we require our providers to protect it.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:22px;margin:34px 0 8px;color:#0f172a;"><i class="fa-solid fa-pen-to-square" style="color:#0188fe;margin-right:8px;"></i>9. Changes to this policy</h2>
<p style="font-size:14.5px;color:#475569;line-height:1.7;margin:0;">If we change how the app handles your information, we will update this page and change the date at the bottom. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.</p>
