[title style="bold-left" text="Warranty Regulations"]

[accordion]

[accordion-item title="1. Warranty Coverage"]

<p>Every AUN projector bought from aunstore.com is covered for <strong>12 months from the date of purchase</strong> against defects in materials and workmanship. Your order confirmation is your proof of purchase &mdash; please keep it.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="2. Nature of Service"]

<p>Warranty service is provided by replacing the defective part. If a specific part (such as the LCD panel, main board, power board or light source) is confirmed defective, a working replacement part is shipped to you from our factory. In such cases, the warranty applies only to the defective component, not the entire product. Replacement is subject to availability of parts.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="3. How to Claim Warranty"]

<p>Contact our support team with your order number and a short phone video (10&ndash;30 seconds) showing the problem. Our engineers diagnose the fault from the video &mdash; many issues are solved with a setting change on the spot. You do <strong>not</strong> need to send the projector back.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="4. Parts Delivery Time"]

<p>Delivery time for a replacement part depends on part availability and your location. We will share the tracking details as soon as the part ships.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="5. Fitting the Part"]

<p>With the part we share a step-by-step video tutorial for your model and part. If you get stuck, message us and our team will guide you. Please do not open the projector for repairs other than as guided by our support team.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="6. Multiple Claims"]

<p>There is no fixed limit on the number of warranty claims during the warranty period. You may claim as long as your warranty is valid.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="7. Data & Software Responsibility"]

<p>We are not responsible for the loss of data, apps, accounts or settings during warranty service. Please note your app logins and settings before any repair.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="8. User Maintenance"]

<p>Projectors require regular user maintenance to ensure proper airflow and cooling. Clean the dust filter (where fitted) and the ventilation areas periodically as described in the user manual.</p>
<p>Failure to maintain proper airflow may cause internal overheating and component damage.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="9. Exclusions"]

<p>The warranty does not cover:</p>
<ul>
<li>Damage caused by water, fire, electric shock, accident or misuse</li>
<li>Opening, repairing or modifying the product other than as guided by our support team</li>
<li>Physical damage (scratches, dents, cracks, broken parts)</li>
<li>Damage caused by non-recommended accessories</li>
<li>Damage caused by overheating due to blocked ventilation, clogged dust filters or lack of regular maintenance</li>
</ul>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="10. Accessories"]

<p>Accessories such as remote controls, cables and adapters are not covered by the warranty.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="11. Out-of-Warranty Service"]

<p>Projector out of warranty? Contact us anyway &mdash; we can still diagnose the fault and supply the replacement part at a quoted cost.</p>

[/accordion-item]

[/accordion]
