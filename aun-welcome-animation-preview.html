Powering on, in order

1 · Power. The green LED catches with a bright flick, then settles and stays on — no slow pulse, that's what the waiting moods do.

2 · Lamp. The beam strikes: narrow and slightly unsteady at first, then it widens and steadies, the way a real lamp comes up. Dust starts riding the light.

3 · Picture. The logo forms on the screen — fades up, settles from slightly small, one soft bloom passes over it.

Then it holds. LED steady, beam breathing, dust drifting, and the eye excited — wider than the calm moods, a happy double-blink three times a loop, a big catchlight plus a second sparkle, and the pupil keeps darting up to admire what it just put on the wall. The body gets a second, faster bounce so it reads as pleased with itself. Loops every 12s with no restart.

Everything here is the existing painter's geometry — screen at 44,14→156,96, lens at (100, 150+bob), the same beam path.
