[section label="faq"]

<p>[aun_head eyebrow="Good to know" icon="fa-circle-question" title="Frequently Asked Questions" sub="Quick answers to what buyers ask us most."]</p>
[row h_align="center"]

[col span="10" span__sm="12"]

[accordion]

[accordion-item title="Can I use it in a bright room?"]

<p>It is designed for dim and dark rooms — close the curtains in the daytime and you get a vivid, cinema-like picture. If your room stays bright all day, consider a higher-brightness model like the U002 Pro or U001 Pro.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How big a screen can I get?"]

<p>From 47&Prime; up to a 150&Prime; picture (1.39 – 4.45 m from the wall) — the further back you place it, the bigger the screen.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Can I watch YouTube or Netflix directly?"]

<p>Yes — it has a built-in Android system with YouTube, Netflix and Prime Video preloaded, plus Wi-Fi screen mirroring (AirPlay &amp; Miracast) from your phone, tablet or laptop. App availability can vary by country.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Will dust affect it?"]

<p>It has a removable, washable dust filter that blocks most dust before it reaches the optics, so build-up is far slower than in unprotected projectors. Clean the filter regularly, and if you ever wash it, let it dry completely before putting it back.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Will it work with the power supply in my country?"]

<p>Yes. The A004 Pro accepts AC 100 – 240 V, so it works on mains power anywhere in the world. If your wall socket is a different shape from the supplied plug, a simple plug adapter is all you need.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How does the warranty work if I live abroad?"]

<p>Every A004 Pro comes with a 1-year AUN warranty, and you never have to ship your projector back. Contact our support team with a short video of the problem. We diagnose it, our factory ships the replacement part straight to your address, and we share a step-by-step video tutorial so you can fit it yourself. See <a href="/warranty/">Warranty &amp; Support</a> for details.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Do you ship to my country?"]

<p>We ship worldwide. Delivery time and shipping cost are shown at checkout once you enter your address. Your country's customs may charge import duties or taxes on arrival — see our <a href="/shipping-policy/">Shipping Policy</a>.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How do I know my projector is genuine?"]

<p>Every genuine AUN projector carries a 12-digit anti-counterfeit code on the bottom of the unit. Enter it at <a href="https://check.aunstore.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">check.aunstore.com</a> to verify it instantly.</p>

[/accordion-item]

[/accordion]
