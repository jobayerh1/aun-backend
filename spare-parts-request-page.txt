The LCD serial, board model number, or a photo of the power board lets us send a part that actually fits &mdash; for example, a heat insulation glass must match your LCD or the picture washes out.</div></details>
<details><summary><span>I lost my invoice number &mdash; can I still request a part?</span><i class="fa-solid fa-chevron-down aun-chev"></i></summary><div class="aun-faq-a">Yes. Our full <a href="https://aun-projector.com.bd/warranty-policy/">warranty policy</a> explains what&rsquo;s covered.</div></details>

You&rsquo;ll see it on the <a href="https://aun-projector.com.bd/spare-parts-status/">tracking page</a> &mdash; each part with its price and a total &mdash; and you simply tap <strong>Approve</strong> to confirm or <strong>Decline</strong>. We only order the part from the factory <em>after</em> you approve, so there are no surprises. Payment instructions are shown right with the quote.</div></details>
<details><summary><span>How long does it take to get a part?</span><i class="fa-solid fa-chevron-down aun-chev"></i></summary><div class="aun-faq-a">If it&rsquo;s in stock, we dispatch quickly. You can follow each part&rsquo;s exact status on the <a href="https://aun-projector.com.bd/spare-parts-status/">tracking page</a>.</div></details>
<details><summary><span>How do I track my request, and what if I lose the number?</span><i class="fa-solid fa-chevron-down aun-chev"></i></summary><div class="aun-faq-a">The moment you submit, we text you your reference number (like SP-2026-0001) with a direct link &mdash; so you won&rsquo;t lose it.
