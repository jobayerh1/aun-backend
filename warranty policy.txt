[title style="bold-left" text="Warranty Regulations (English)"]

[accordion]

[accordion-item title="1. Warranty Coverage"]

<p>The warranty applies only to products where “Warranty” is clearly mentioned in the invoice at the time of purchase. Products without a warranty mention are not eligible.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="2. Nature of Service"]

<p>Warranty service includes the repair or replacement of defective parts. If a specific part (such as an LCD screen, motherboard, power board, or lamp) is confirmed defective, it will be replaced with a functional part. In such cases, the warranty applies only to the defective component, not the entire product. Replacement is subject to availability of parts. Need a specific part? You can <a href="https://aun-projector.com.bd/spare-parts/" style="color:#0188fe; font-weight:bold;">request a spare part online</a>.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="3. Repair Timeline"]

<p>Repair time depends on the availability of spare parts and the nature of the fault. If the required parts are in stock, repairs are usually completed within 2–3 working days. However, if spare parts need to be imported, the process may take 45 days or more.</p>
<p>Customers can check their <a href="https://aun-projector.com.bd/repair-status/" style="color:#0188fe; font-weight:bold;">repair progress</a> online anytime using their ticket or invoice number.</p>


[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="4. Multiple Claims"]

<p>There is no fixed limit on the number of warranty claims during the warranty period. Customers may avail service as long as the warranty is valid.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="5. Data & Software Responsibility"]

<p>We are not responsible for the loss of data, software, or configuration during warranty service. Customers should back up their data before submitting the product.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="6. User Maintenance"]

<p>Projectors require regular user maintenance to ensure proper airflow and cooling. Customers are required to clean the dust filter and ventilation areas periodically as described in the user manual and on the product label.</p>
<p>Failure to maintain proper airflow may cause internal overheating and component damage.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="7. Exclusions"]

<p>Warranty will not be valid under the following circumstances:</p>
<ul>
<li>Damage caused by water, fire, electric shock, accident, or misuse</li>
<li>Opening or repairing the product by unauthorized persons</li>
<li>Physical damage (scratches, dents, broken parts)</li>
<li>Use of non-recommended accessories causing damage</li>
<li>Damage caused by overheating due to blocked ventilation, clogged dust filters, or lack of regular maintenance</li>
</ul>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="8. Accessories"]

<p>Accessories such as remote controls, cables, adapters, or incomplete products are not covered by warranty.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="9. Non-Warranty Services"]

<p>Services outside warranty coverage will be provided at a cost agreed upon between the company and the customer.</p>
<p>Non-warranty repairs can also be tracked online via the same repair status portal once a ticket is created.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="10. How to Claim Warranty & Service Center Address"]

<p>If your projector requires service, please contact our support team to create a repair ticket. You can drop off the projector or send it via courier to our official service center &mdash; <a href="https://aun-projector.com.bd/send-projector/" style="color:#0188fe; font-weight:bold;">see how to courier it in &rarr;</a> <strong>Customers are responsible for courier charges when sending the product.</strong></p>
