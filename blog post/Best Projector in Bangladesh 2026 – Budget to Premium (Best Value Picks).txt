<h2>How to Choose the Best Projector in Bangladesh</h2>

<h3>1) Brightness (ANSI Lumens) matters more than “LED Lumens”</h3>
<p>For Bangladesh homes, ambient light is common — so match the model to your room instead of chasing spec-sheet numbers. One honest rule first: <strong>spec-sheet lumens across the whole industry are manufacturer light-source ratings</strong>, so real on-screen brightness is always lower, for every brand equally (full explanation in our <a href="/projector-brightness-explained/">brightness guide</a>). As a simple room guide:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Dark room / bedroom:</strong> any AUN Full HD home model shines — A004 Pro, A005, A45 Pro</li>
  <li><strong>Living room / some light:</strong> step up in brightness and close the curtains in daytime</li>
  <li><strong>Bright room / classroom:</strong> our brightest models — U002 Pro or U001 Pro — plus light control or an ALR screen</li>
</ul>

<h3>2) Resolution: 720p vs 1080p</h3>
<p>720p is fine for casual viewing and smaller screen sizes. For sharp text, presentations, and cinema-like detail, <strong>native 1080p</strong> is the better long-term choice.</p>

<h3>3) Android vs Android TV</h3>
<p><strong>Android TV</strong> is designed for TV streaming apps and usually provides better compatibility. <strong>Standard Android</strong> is flexible and budget-friendly; you can use built-in apps or connect a Fire TV / Chromecast.</p>

<h2>Important: ANSI Lumens vs Marketing Lumens</h2>

<p>When buying a projector in Bangladesh, be careful about brightness specifications. Some listings show very high "LED lumens" or unverified numbers.</p>

<p>For accurate comparison, understand the two scales: <strong>ANSI lumens</strong> (a standardized measurement of on-screen brightness — what reviewers use) and <strong>rated lumens</strong> (the manufacturer's light-source figure printed on spec sheets — always much higher, for every brand). Never compare one brand's ANSI figure against another brand's rated figure.</p>
