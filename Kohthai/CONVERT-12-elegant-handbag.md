## ⭐ This description is not yours, and that is an SEO problem

Look at what is in it:

- *"the elegant and durable **Miniministore** handbag"* — Miniministore is a **marketplace seller
  brand**. This is the third supplier brand I have found in your copy, after LEMON KOKO twice.
- *"boasts a high load-carrying capacity"*
- *"makes a wonderful gift for **Mother's Day, Valentine's Day**, or any special occasion"*
- the dimensions spelled out in prose: *"Measuring 30 cm long, 13 cm wide, and 21 cm high"*

⭐⭐ **That is Amazon listing copy, pasted whole.** The gift-occasions sentence and the
dimensions-in-prose are the tells — no shop writes that about its own product; marketplace sellers
write it to hit search filters.

**Why it matters beyond tidiness:** if this text was lifted from a marketplace listing, **it is
almost certainly running word-for-word on dozens of other sites.** Google treats that as duplicate
content, and the site with the copy that appears everywhere is not the one that ranks. You are
paying for Rank Math and then handing it text it cannot win with.

It also explains the pattern across your catalogue: **the copy describes the supplier's product,
not yours** — which is why colours, materials and pocket counts keep failing to match.

The rewrite below is original text, written from your own photograph.

Every conversion so far has been me finding the gap between **the supplier's description** and
**your actual bag**. Wrong material, wrong colours, wrong pocket count, wrong strap, someone
