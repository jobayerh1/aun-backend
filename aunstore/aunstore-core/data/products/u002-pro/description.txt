[section label="faq"]

<p>[aun_head eyebrow="Good to know" icon="fa-circle-question" title="Frequently Asked Questions" sub="Quick answers to what buyers ask us most."]</p>
[row h_align="center"]

[col span="10" span__sm="12"]

[accordion]

[accordion-item title="Can I use it in a bright room?"]

<p>It looks its best in dim or dark rooms. In the daytime, just close the curtains for a vivid picture — like any home projector, direct sunlight will wash out the image.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How far should I place it for a big screen?"]

<p>Anywhere from 1.4 m to 5.7 m, giving you a 50&Prime; to 200&Prime; picture. As a rule of thumb, around 2.8 m gives you a 100&Prime; cinema screen.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Can I watch YouTube or Netflix directly?"]

<p>Yes — it runs Android 13 with preloaded apps like YouTube, Netflix and Prime Video, plus Wi-Fi screen mirroring (AirPlay &amp; Miracast) from your phone. App availability can vary by country.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Will dust affect it?"]

<p>Its optical engine is fully sealed and dust-proof, so dust cannot reach the picture-critical optics. No dust spots, no filter cleaning — maintenance-free by design.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Will it work with the power supply in my country?"]

<p>Yes. The U002 Pro accepts AC 100 – 240 V, so it works on mains power anywhere in the world. If your wall socket is a different shape from the supplied plug, a simple plug adapter is all you need.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How does the warranty work if I live abroad?"]

<p>Every U002 Pro comes with a 1-year AUN warranty, and you never have to ship your projector back. Contact our support team with a short video of the problem. We diagnose it, our factory ships the replacement part straight to your address, and we share a step-by-step video tutorial so you can fit it yourself. See <a href="/warranty/">Warranty &amp; Support</a> for details.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Do you ship to my country?"]

<p>We ship worldwide. Delivery time and shipping cost are shown at checkout once you enter your address. Your country's customs may charge import duties or taxes on arrival — see our <a href="/shipping-policy/">Shipping Policy</a>.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How do I know my projector is genuine?"]

<p>Every genuine AUN projector carries a 12-digit anti-counterfeit code on the bottom of the unit. Enter it at <a href="https://check.aunstore.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">check.aunstore.com</a> to verify it instantly.</p>

[/accordion-item]

[/accordion]
