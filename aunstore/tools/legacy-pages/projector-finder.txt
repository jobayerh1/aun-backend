<details open><summary><span>How do I choose the right projector for my room?</span><i class="fa-solid fa-chevron-down aun-chev"></i></summary><div class="aun-faq-a">Use the Smart Finder above. In 5 quick steps it asks about your <strong>use case, room lighting, screen size, projector placement (throw distance) and budget</strong> &mdash; then recommends the best-matched AUN model and explains exactly why it fits.</div></details>
<details><summary><span>How much brightness do I need?</span><i class="fa-solid fa-chevron-down aun-chev"></i></summary><div class="aun-faq-a">For a dark room, a lower-brightness model is fine; for a room with daylight or office lighting, you'll want a brighter projector. The Finder factors this in automatically based on the lighting you choose.</div></details>
<details><summary><span>Does projector placement (throw distance) matter?</span><i class="fa-solid fa-chevron-down aun-chev"></i></summary><div class="aun-faq-a">Yes. How far the projector sits from the screen decides the picture size and which lens suits you. The Finder asks where it will sit &mdash; very close (under 1.5 m), a normal room (1.5&ndash;4 m) or a large hall (4 m+) &mdash; and recommends a short-throw lens where it's needed. To see the exact picture size, open the <strong>Screen Size Calculator</strong> tab on any product page.</div></details>
