[title style="center" text="FAQs & Notes"]

[accordion]

[accordion-item title="What is the minimum purchase amount for EMI?"]

<p>EMI is available for purchases above BDT 5,000.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="What is the maximum tenure available?"]

<p>We offer 0% EMI for up to 6 months.</p>


[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How does the EMI reflect on my credit card?"]

<p>The bank will block your full purchase amount initially, then release it gradually as you pay your monthly installments.</p>
<p><em>For example, if you have made a 3-month EMI purchase of BDT 20,000 and your credit limit is BDT 1,00,000 then your bank will block your credit limit by BDT 20,000 and thus your available credit limit after the purchase will only be BDT 80,000. As and when you pay your EMI every month, your credit limit will be released accordingly.</em></p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Which banks are supported?"]

<p>34 leading banks in Bangladesh, including DBBL, City Bank, EBL, Standard Chartered, Trust Bank, and more.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Who should I contact if my EMI is not reflected?"]

<p>Please contact your card issuing bank if your EMI conversion doesn’t appear within 7 working days.</p>

[/accordion-item]
