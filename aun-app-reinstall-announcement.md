# One-time re-install announcement

Paste into **AUN App → Settings → Announcement**. It shows as a card near the top of the app's
Home screen. Remove it about a week after everyone has moved over.

⚠️ **Publish the new signed APK to the website FIRST**, then set the announcement. Otherwise people
uninstall and find the old build still sitting on the download page.

---

---

### Why it is worded this way

- **Says "security update", not "signing key".** True, and it explains *why* without a technical
  detour a customer can't act on.
- **Leads with the action.** Uninstall → install. That is the only thing they must actually do.
- **Kills the fear immediately.** The real worry is "will I lose my warranty / my repair?" — so the
  reassurance sits in the same sentence, not at the bottom.
- **No version numbers.** They go stale, and the customer doesn't need them.

### Also worth doing

- Put the same line on the website download page for a week — people who uninstall before opening
  the app never see the in-app card.
- If someone reports **"App not installed"**, that is exactly this: they tried to install over the
  old copy. Tell them to uninstall first.
