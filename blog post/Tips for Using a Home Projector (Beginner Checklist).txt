<h2 id="room">1) Room & light control</h2>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Night/dark rooms</strong> give the best contrast. Dim lamps away from the screen; block direct light.</li>
          <li><strong>Daytime/bright rooms:</strong> close blinds, reduce reflections, and consider a higher-gain/ALR screen.</li>
          <li>Avoid placing lights that shine <em>onto</em> the screen — aim lighting to the sides or behind seating.</li>
        </ul>
<p>For brighter living rooms in Bangladesh, choosing a higher-brightness model like the U002 Pro or U001 Pro from our updated <a href="/projector-price/">projector price list</a> can significantly improve picture clarity — and see our <a href="/projector-brightness-explained/">honest brightness guide</a> to understand what the lumens numbers really mean.</p>

        <h2 id="screen">2) Screen or wall?</h2>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Best:</strong> matte white fixed or pull-down screens for dark rooms.</li>
          <li><strong>Bright rooms:</strong> higher-gain or ALR screens help fight ambient light.</li>
          <li><strong>No screen?</strong> Use a smooth, matte light-grey wall (not glossy).</li>
          <li>Pick a size that fits your room: see our <a href="/best-screen-size-for-home-theater/">Best Screen Size guide</a> for distance math.</li>
        </ul>

        <h2 id="picture">3) Picture modes & color</h2>
        <ul>
          <li>Start with <strong>Cinema/Movie</strong> (dark rooms) or <strong>Standard</strong> (mixed light).</li>
          <li>Set <strong>Color Temperature</strong> to Warm/Neutral; reduce <strong>Sharpness</strong> to avoid edge halos.</li>
          <li>Sports: enable mild motion smoothing (Low/Medium). See <a href="/football-cricket-on-projector-bd/">football & cricket tips</a>.</li>
        </ul>

        <h2 id="hdmi">4) HDMI basics (stable signal)</h2>
        <ul>
          <li>Use a short, quality cable first; set your source to <strong>1080p @ 60 Hz</strong> for compatibility.</li>
          <li>Power order for handshake: connect HDMI → turn on <strong>projector</strong> → then <strong>source</strong>.</li>
          <li>Trouble? Follow our full guide: <a href="/no-signal-on-projector-hdmi/">Fix “No Signal” on Projector (HDMI)</a>.</li>
        </ul>

        <h2 id="sound">5) Sound tips</h2>
        <ul>
          <li>Built-in speakers are fine for casual use; a <strong>soundbar</strong> is the best “first upgrade.”</li>
          <li>Place the soundbar below the screen; enable dialogue enhancement / night mode if available.</li>
          <li>For larger rooms: 2.1/3.1 bookshelf or tower speakers for fuller impact.</li>
        </ul>

        <h2 id="care">6) Care & maintenance</h2>
        <ul>
          <li>Keep vents clear; use a dust cover when not in use.</li>
          <li>Clean the screen and outer lens gently (no harsh solvents). Full steps in <a href="/how-to-clean-the-screen-of-lcd-projectors/">our cleaning guide</a>.</li>
          <li>Avoid heavy keystone correction; physically align projector height and center when possible.</li>
        </ul>

<div class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question">What picture mode should I use for movies?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer">
<p><strong>Cinema/Movie</strong> in dark rooms, <strong>Standard</strong> in mixed light. Warm/Neutral color temperature, lower sharpness.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question">Do I need a screen or is a wall okay?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer">
<p>A screen is best; for casual viewing a smooth matte light-grey wall works. Bright rooms benefit from ALR/higher-gain screens.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question">Why does my projector say “No Signal”?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer">
<p>Often HDMI handshake or resolution issues. Try 1080p 60 Hz, another cable/port, and follow our full guide linked above.</p>
