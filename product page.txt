[aun_head eyebrow="Good to know" icon="fa-circle-question" title="Frequently Asked Questions" sub="Quick answers to what buyers ask us most."]

[row h_align="center"]

[col span="10" span__sm="12"]

[accordion]

[accordion-item title="Can I use it in a bright room?"]

It shines in dim or dark rooms. In the daytime, just close the curtains for a vivid picture — like any home projector, full direct sunlight will wash out the image.

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="What does the lumens rating actually mean?"]

The brightness figure on the spec sheet is the manufacturer's official rating, measured at the light source — the convention used across the projector industry by virtually every brand. The brightness you actually see on screen is always lower than the rated figure, for every projector on the market, because light is lost passing through the lens system. That's why we describe every model by room type instead, and show real unedited projection photos on this page — so you judge with your own eyes, not with a number. Want the full story? Read our honest guide: <a href="/projector-brightness-explained/">Projector Brightness, Explained</a>.

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="How far should I place it for a big screen?"]

Anywhere from 1.4 m to 5.7 m, giving you a 50&Prime; to 200&Prime; picture. As a rule of thumb, around 2.8 m gives you a 100&Prime; cinema screen.

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Can I watch YouTube or Netflix directly?"]

Yes — it runs Android 13 with preloaded apps like YouTube, Netflix and Prime Video, plus Wi-Fi screen mirroring (AirPlay &amp; Miracast) from your phone.

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Will dust damage it in Bangladesh?"]

The U002 Pro is built for Bangladesh's dusty conditions — its optical engine is fully sealed and dust-proof, so dust cannot reach the picture-critical optics. No dust spots, no filter cleaning, maintenance-free by design.

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="What about warranty and service?"]

Every unit comes with a 1-year official AUN warranty and nationwide service support. Register your warranty online after purchase for the fastest claims.

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Can I pay with EMI, bKash or cash on delivery?"]

Yes — we offer 0% EMI, bKash/Nagad, cards, and cash on delivery across Bangladesh.

[/accordion-item]

[/accordion]
