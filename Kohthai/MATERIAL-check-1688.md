# Is the Rodeo Crossbody vegetable-tanned leather or PU?

I could not open `detail.1688.com/offer/869950002519.html` — 1688 served a **"Captcha
Interception"** page to both my direct fetch and the browser. I don't work around CAPTCHAs, so
you will have to read the listing. Here is exactly what to look at.

---

## Test 1 — the price. You already know this answer.

**What did you pay per unit?**

| Wholesale unit price on 1688 | What it can be |
|---|---|
| under ¥100 (~৳1,700) | **PU.** No exceptions at this size. |
| ¥100 – ¥180 | PU, or split/bonded leather. Not vegetable-tanned. |
| ¥250+ | Now real cowhide is plausible |

Vegetable tanning takes **weeks** in bark pits rather than a day in a chrome drum, and the hide
has to be good enough to show. That cost cannot land in a ¥60 bag. **If you paid a normal
handbag-factory price, it is PU and no further checking is needed.**

## Test 2 — the listing's spec table

On the 1688 page, scroll to **产品属性** (product attributes) and find the row **材质** (material).
Then match what it says:

**These mean PU / synthetic:**

| Chinese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PU` · `PU皮` | PU |
| `人造革` | artificial leather |
| `合成革` | synthetic leather |
| `仿真皮` | imitation leather |
| `超纤` · `超纤皮` | microfibre synthetic |

**These mean real leather:**

| Chinese | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `真皮` | genuine leather |
| `头层牛皮` | top-grain cowhide |
| `二层牛皮` | split cowhide (real, lower grade) |
| **`植鞣革`** · **`植鞣皮`** | **vegetable-tanned leather — the actual term** |

⚠️ **`荔枝纹`, `十字纹`, `纳帕` are grain patterns, not materials.** They appear on both PU and real
leather and tell you nothing. A listing that names only a grain pattern has not told you what the
bag is made of.

⭐ **If the word `植鞣` does not appear anywhere on that page, the bag is not vegetable-tanned
leather** — regardless of what the title of your own listing says. That word is the whole claim.

## Test 3 — pick one up off your shelf. This beats the listing.

Factory listings on 1688 are frequently aspirational, so the bag in your hand outranks the page.
Two non-destructive checks:

**a) Look for a repeating grain.** Put the bag under good light and follow the grain across a
large flat panel. **Machine-embossed PU repeats** — the same cluster of pores recurs every few
centimetres, like wallpaper. Real leather never repeats; its pores are irregular and it will show
natural variation, healed scars or stretch marks near the edges.

**b) Look at a cut edge or the underside of a strap.** Real leather is fibrous the whole way
through — same material, suede-like on the reverse. PU shows a distinct sandwich: a smooth
plastic film sitting on a **woven fabric backing** you can see the weave of.

A third, if you have a sample you don't mind marking: a drop of water on veg-tan **soaks in and
darkens** within a minute. On PU it beads and wipes off with no mark.

## What to do with the answer

**If it is PU** (which I expect):

1. Change the product title — drop "Vegetable Tanned Leather".
2. Use `Material: PU Leather` in the Key Feature field and
   `material="PU leather with a glossy finish"` in `[kt_details]`.
3. **Check your other 20 products for the same phrase.** If it came from a supplier listing on
   one, it may have come from supplier listings on others.

**If it genuinely is `植鞣革`:**

1. Keep the title — and make far more of it than you currently do. It is a real, defensible
   differentiator that none of your competitors can copy cheaply, and right now it is buried in a
   Materials tab.
2. Fix the description paragraph that calls the same bag "medium-soft PU".
3. Use `Material: Vegetable Tanned Leather`, and drop "glossy" from the short description —
   veg-tan is matte, and calling it glossy undersells the thing that makes it worth more.

**If the listing is ambiguous:** message the supplier on 1688 and ask
**「这个包是真皮还是PU？是植鞣革吗？」** — *"Is this bag real leather or PU? Is it
vegetable-tanned?"* Keep their written reply.

---

## Why I am being this careful about one word

Your product title is your Google result and your first promise. "Vegetable Tanned Leather" on a
PU bag is a false material claim — the same family as a fake review or an invented "was" price,
and worse in one respect: **a customer can discover it by touch, then photograph it.** Under your
own 7-day policy, "not as described" is a return you pay for, and a public one.

It is a five-minute check and it only has to be done once.
