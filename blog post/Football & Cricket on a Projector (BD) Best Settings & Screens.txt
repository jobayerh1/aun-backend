<h2>Picture settings for smoother motion</h2>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Mode:</strong> Sports or Cinema (whichever looks natural)</li>
          <li><strong>Sharpness:</strong> reduce to avoid edge halos</li>
          <li><strong>Motion/Interpolation:</strong> Low or Medium; avoid Soap Opera effect</li>
          <li><strong>Color temp:</strong> Warm/Neutral</li>
        </ul>
