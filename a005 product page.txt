[section label="faq"]

<p>[aun_head eyebrow="Good to know" icon="fa-circle-question" title="Frequently Asked Questions" sub="Quick answers to what buyers ask us most."]</p>
[row h_align="center"]

[col span="10" span__sm="12"]

[accordion]

[accordion-item title="My room is small — will it still give a big picture?"]

<p>Yes — that is exactly what the A005 is built for. Its 1.1:1 short-throw lens delivers a 100&Prime; picture from just 2.4 m, so even a compact bedroom becomes a cinema. Full range: 50&Prime; to 150&Prime; from 1.16 – 3.5 m.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Can I use it in a bright room?"]

<p>The A005 is designed for dim and dark rooms — close the curtains in the daytime and you get a vivid, cinema-like picture. If your room stays bright all day, consider a higher-brightness model like the U002 Pro or U001 Pro.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="What does the lumens rating actually mean?"]

<p>The brightness figure on the spec sheet is the manufacturer's official rating, measured at the light source — the convention used across the projector industry by virtually every brand. The brightness you actually see on screen is always lower than the rated figure, for every projector on the market, because light is lost passing through the lens system. That's why we describe every model by room type instead, and show real unedited projection photos on this page — so you judge with your own eyes, not with a number. Want the full story? Read our honest guide: <a href="/projector-brightness-explained/">Projector Brightness, Explained</a>.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Can I watch YouTube or Netflix directly?"]

<p>Yes — it runs Android 12 with YouTube, Netflix and Prime Video preloaded, plus Wi-Fi screen mirroring (AirPlay &amp; Miracast) from your phone, tablet or laptop.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Will dust damage it in Bangladesh?"]

<p>Dust is the biggest enemy of projectors in Bangladesh — and the A005 is built with that in mind. Its removable dust filter blocks most dust before it reaches the optics, so build-up is far slower than in unprotected projectors. Clean the filter regularly, and if you ever wash it, let it dry completely before putting it back.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="What about warranty and service?"]

<p>Every unit comes with a 1-year official AUN warranty. If anything goes wrong, contact our support team and send the unit to our service center — we'll repair it and courier it back to you, anywhere in Bangladesh. You can even track your repair status live on our website. Bought from our online store? Your warranty is registered automatically. Bought from an authorized dealer or physical shop? Just register your warranty online to activate it.</p>

[/accordion-item]
[accordion-item title="Can I pay with EMI, bKash or cash on delivery?"]

<p>Yes — we offer 0% EMI, bKash/Nagad, cards, and cash on delivery across Bangladesh.</p>

[/accordion-item]
